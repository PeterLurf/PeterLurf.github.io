Peter Lu

I'm a first-year math student at the University of Toronto, interested in mechanistic interpretability: working out what neural networks are doing on the inside. Lately I've been studying looped language models, and whether they can tell you about the reasoning they do in their hidden states.

In 2026 I was on Team Canada at the International AI Olympiad in Slovenia, where I won a silver medal. In high school I started the math, STEM and AI clubs at Unionville, which taught over 1,500 students.

Research
