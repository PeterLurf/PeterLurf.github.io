Research

  • Endpoint Self-Report in Looped Language Models I injected concept vectors into each loop of a recurrent-depth model, then asked it which sentence had been changed. The answer is easy to read off its activations (96.9–100% by subtracting a clean run, 99.5% with a LoRA readout), but the model's own answers are at chance. Mechanistic interpretability
  • Latent Reasoning Can a language model reason in its hidden states without writing out its steps? On permutation tracking, GPT-2 carries the state in latent thoughts and stays exact out to 2,048 steps after training on 128, at 0.3–0.6× the compute of writing the state out. In progress
  • Reading the Decoder: Colluding Monitors Reuse Their Own Evidence Heads When an LLM code-review monitor colludes with the model it's checking, about 20 attention heads that normally carry evidence of a backdoor carry the hidden signal as evidence of clean code instead. Patching them undoes 85% of the collusion, and a linear probe on the monitor catches attacks the monitor lets through. Apart Research AI Collusion Sprint, October 2026
  • Linebreak Heads in Open-Weight LMs Claude 3.5 Haiku decides where to break a line with a few attention heads that compare a character count with the previous line's length. 15 of 16 open models I checked (Gemma-2, Qwen2.5, Pythia, GPT-2) build the same heads, and clamping the count makes them break lines 2–8 characters late. October 2026
  • BlazeShield: RL for Wildfire Containment Q-learning to place firebreaks in a PDE wildfire simulation. SproutHacks
