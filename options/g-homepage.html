Bio

I'm a first-year math student at the University of Toronto working on mechanistic interpretability: figuring out what neural networks actually compute, with activation patching, probing, steering and circuit analysis. Lately that means recurrent-depth (looped) language models, and what their latent reasoning does and doesn't expose.

In 2026 I won a silver medal for Canada at the International AI Olympiad in Slovenia. Before university I ran math, STEM and AI clubs that taught more than 1,500 students.
