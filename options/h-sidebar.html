Research

The highlighted one is where I'd start.

Endpoint Self-Report in Looped Language Models

I injected concept vectors into each loop of a recurrent-depth LM and asked the model which sentence had been changed. Subtracting a clean run recovers the answer 96.9 to 100% of the time and a LoRA readout gets 99.5%, but the model itself stays at chance. The information is there; the model doesn't use it.
